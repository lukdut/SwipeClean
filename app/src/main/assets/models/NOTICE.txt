The ONNX graph is distributed unchanged. SwipeClean applies RGB bilinear resizing,
white alpha compositing and [-1, 1] channel normalization, then stores a per-vector
int8 quantization of the pooled 768-dimensional output. These vectors are used
only to compare images; the model does not decide whether a photo is valuable.
